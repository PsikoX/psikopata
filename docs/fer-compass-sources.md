# FER compass references

- Historical compass roses sometimes placed a Christian cross at east, pointing towards Jerusalem from Mediterranean charts; a fleur-de-lis commonly marked north. Source: [Compass rose, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_rose), including the Cantino planisphere compass example (1502).
- The map inset is an actual 1865 Ordnance Survey of Jerusalem, led by Charles William Wilson. Its [Wikimedia Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:1865_Ordnance_Survey_of_Jerusalem_Old_City_full_map.jpg) is marked public domain. The website uses an optimized copy at `assets/images/jerusalem-survey-1865.webp`.
- The brass compass photograph at `assets/images/fer-compass.webp` is an original generated artistic reconstruction. It is not a photograph of a historical museum object, and its background map is illustrative. The site image alternative text explicitly calls it a reconstruction.
- F, E and R and their convergence are the PSIKOPAPA/FER concept; the compass is a visual metaphor for direction, not a historical claim about FER or a religious artifact.
